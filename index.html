Privacy Policy for SpendWise

SpendWise does not collect or share any personal user data.

The app may request permissions such as Camera or Storage for essential features like taking bill photos or exporting reports, but no data is stored or sent to any server.

All financial records stay only on your device or in your connected cloud account (if you enabled backup).

We respect your privacy and do not track or sell your information.
